Studio Eclectik is a design studio founded by Emma & Katharina to bring brand, space and object together under one roof.

We create scenography, visual merchandising and bespoke pieces that turn ideas into physical experiences. We recognise that each project is unique and tailor the disciplines to each project’s needs rather than fitting every project to one method. Whether the brief is a window, an installation or a launch, our approach is the same: strategic thinking, hands-on making and a story worth remembering.
